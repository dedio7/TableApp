# Privacy Policy for DailyPulse

**Effective Date: June 11, 2026**

DailyPulse ("we", "us", or "our") operates the DailyPulse Android application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## 1. Data Collection and Use
We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

### 1.1 Location Data
We use your geographic location (GPS) to provide accurate local weather forecasts. This data is sent directly to the Open-Meteo API (https://open-meteo.com) to retrieve weather information. We do not store your location history on our servers.

### 1.2 Calendar Data
DailyPulse accesses your local Android Calendar to display your scheduled events on the dashboard. You may also add, edit, or delete events through the app. These changes are synced directly with your Google Calendar or local calendar provider. We do not transmit your calendar data to any external servers other than your chosen calendar provider.

## 2. Data Storage
All application settings and cached weather data are stored locally on your device using Android DataStore. We do not maintain any external database of your personal information.

## 3. Third-Party Services
We use the following third-party services:
- **Open-Meteo**: For weather data.
- **Google Calendar API**: Via the Android System Provider for event management.
- **RSS Feeds**: To provide news updates (the app acts as a reader only).
